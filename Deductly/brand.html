Brand sheet

The Deductly brand

The logo, colours and type used across the Deductly web app. Smart Expenses. Simple Refunds.

Logo

One mark, three sizes

Primary logo The icon and name without the tagline. For site headers, emails and documents at 100 px wide or more.
On Deductly Green Use the light versions on Deductly Green, Deep Green or Ink. The full logo works at 200 px wide or more.
Icon The D with its arrow. For anything under 100 px wide, such as headers on phones and profile images.
App icon The light icon on Deductly Green, with a corner radius of about 22% of the width.

Favicon sizes The icon stays readable down to 16 px, so it also serves as the browser tab icon.

Clear space

Keep a quarter of the icon's height free on every side of the logo. No text, edges or other graphics inside it.

Backgrounds

Place the logo on Paper, white or a solid green. On photos, use a plain area or put the logo on a solid panel.

The tagline

Smart Expenses. Simple Refunds. appears only inside the full logo. Elsewhere, write it as plain text in Ink.
