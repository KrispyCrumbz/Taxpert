The ATO's basic rules

Every deduction has to pass three tests

Deductly asks all three for each expense you add, so your plan is ready if the ATO ever asks about it.

You paid for it

You spent the money yourself and your employer didn't pay you back.

It earned your income

The expense relates directly to earning your income. If it's part private, you claim only the work portion.

You can prove it

You have a record, such as a receipt, an invoice or a logbook.

Deduction categories

Sorted under the same labels you'll see in myTax

Each category comes with a plain-language guide and a reminder of what to keep.

D1 · D2

Car and work travel

Trips between work sites and travel that is part of the job.

Keep a logbook or a record of your work trips.

D3

Clothing and laundry

Uniforms and protective gear, plus cleaning them. Everyday clothes don't count.

Keep receipts and a note of how often you wash.

D4

Self-education

Courses and study that relate to the job you have now.

Keep invoices and the course details.

D5

Other work-related

Working from home, tools, phone and internet use, and union fees.

Keep an hours log and your receipts.

D8

Gifts and donations

Gifts of $2 or more to deductible gift recipients.

Keep the receipt from each charity.

D9

Managing tax affairs

Tax agent fees and other costs of dealing with the ATO.

Keep the invoice and note the date you paid it.
